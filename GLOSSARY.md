# Cadence

An online course platform where learners buy courses and work through their lessons.

## Language

### Purchases

**Refund**:
A learner's request to get their money back for a purchase, which our team reviews by hand and then approves or declines.
_Avoid_: Chargeback, reversal, cancellation

**Requested**:
A refund the learner has submitted that our team has not yet approved or declined.
_Avoid_: Pending, open

**Approved**:
A refund our team has accepted, fixing the amount sent back to the learner.

**Declined**:
A refund our team has turned down; no money goes back to the learner.

**Refund window**:
The fourteen calendar days after a purchase during which the learner may request a refund.
_Avoid_: Cancel window, cooling-off period
